About CeyBEC Campus

Early Beginnings

CeyBEC Campus was established in response to the growing demand for specialized education in bio-engineering and e-commerce. Recognizing the need for a holistic approach to education that integrates these two rapidly evolving fields, a group of visionary educators and industry experts came together to create an institution that would prepare students for the challenges of the modern job market.

Incorporation & Expansion

CeyBEC Campus was incorporated as an educational institute under Sri Lanka’s Company Act No. 7. This milestone marked the beginning of its journey towards becoming a leading educational institution in Sri Lanka. The campus initially focused on offering foundational courses designed to provide students with the necessary skills and knowledge to excel in higher education and their future careers.

Global Partnerships

To enhance the quality of education and provide students with international opportunities, CeyBEC Campus established partnerships with renowned institutions worldwide. Notably, it became the Sri Lanka Student Recruitment Partner of 850+ Universities and a partner campus of ICA University in New Zealand. These partnerships have allowed CeyBEC Campus to offer globally recognized qualifications and facilitate seamless transitions for students seeking further education abroad.

Programs Offered

Business Management, Civil Engineering, Information Communication Technology (ICT), Early Childhood Education, Pharmacy Technician, Bio-Technology, Robotics, Sport Science, Logistic and Supply Chain Management, and Tourism.
